Privacy Policy.

How this website handles information when you use a tool or submit an inquiry.

UPDATED NOVEMBER 10, 2026

Information you submit

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, inquiry topic, and message. The company field is required for project inquiries and optional for privacy requests. You can also provide your website, market, advertising channels, and monthly ad spend range. If you open the form from a tool, you can choose whether to include its result. The site records when the inquiry was submitted and assigns it a reference.

How inquiries are used and stored

Submitted details are used to respond to your request and follow up on the related discussion. Submissions are stored in a private inquiry inbox and sent to the site owner by email. The form does not subscribe you to a mailing list. Records are kept as needed to handle the inquiry and related follow-up. The inbox currently has no automatic expiry for inquiry records.

Tools and browser storage

The calculators, URL builder, and AI prompt builder run in your browser. If you choose to continue from a tool to the inquiry form, the site temporarily stores a summary and a suggested topic in the current browser tab. The result is included in your submission only if the attachment box is selected. The tab’s saved summary is cleared after a successful submission or when that browser session ends.

The prompt builder prepares text for you to copy. If you paste it into an external AI assistant or use another linked tool, that service handles any information you provide under its own privacy policy.

Google Analytics

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics uses cookies to measure visits, traffic sources, and interactions such as clicks on outbound links. The site uses these reports to understand how visitors use its content and tools. Your analytics choice is saved in your browser. Use Analytics settings in the footer to change it at any time. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Hosting and sign-in

OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare host the site. Supabase stores new inquiries, and Cloudflare delivers inquiry details to the site owner’s Gmail inbox. Earlier submissions remain in Cloudflare storage. The providers may process technical information, such as an IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the site. Where ChatGPT sign-in is required, OpenAI handles authentication and may use session cookies for that purpose.

WhatsApp

If you message me on WhatsApp, your phone number and messages are processed by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. They are used to respond to your request. Keep passwords and customer records out of the chat.

  • WhatsApp Privacy Policy

Privacy questions and requests

For a question about your information or to request access, correction, or deletion, use the privacy request form. If it relates to a past inquiry, include your inquiry reference if available. Keep passwords and customer records out of the message.
