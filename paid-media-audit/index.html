02 / FIT AND SCOPE

When an audit helps.

The audit is for teams already running Google or Meta ads that want to understand weak lead quality, rising acquisition costs, or where to put their next dollar.

Start with one account and one primary offer. If you have aggregate CRM outcomes, they help connect campaign activity to sales. The review uses the available data and flags what needs better measurement.
