01 / WHAT I DO

Three ways I can help.

01

Media buying

Setup, management, and optimization of Google, Meta, and LinkedIn campaigns. Budgets, bids, audiences, and search terms are reviewed against lead quality, not only against cost per lead.

02

Ad creation

Ad copy and creative for each platform, written around one offer and one landing page so the ad matches what the buyer was looking for and the page keeps the promise.

03

Tracking and lead quality

Conversion tracking and sales feedback, so campaigns can optimize for qualified leads instead of raw form fills, and you can see what a qualified lead costs.

03
