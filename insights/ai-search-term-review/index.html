Use AI to review search terms without losing control

Build a repeatable review workflow with an input template and a human approval step.

NOVEMBER 5, 2026

A search term export is a useful place to start when a B2B campaign is buying the wrong traffic. An AI assistant can help organize the review, provided you give it the offer, the target buyer, and a way to flag ambiguous cases.

Prepare a short input

List the product, audience, markets, and terms you need to protect. Then add the search terms and aggregate measures like clicks and spend. Remove personal information, customer names, and anything your organization treats as confidential. Include the reporting period and currency so the output has a clear scope.

Ask for a review queue

Use four labels: high intent, research, irrelevant, and unclear. Ask for an explanation, confidence level, and a suggested next action for each term. When the assistant proposes an exclusion, ask for any relevant meanings or offers it could accidentally block.

Check the output before changing an account

Read every suggested exclusion against your positive keywords and landing pages. Check language variants and possible uses of ambiguous terms. Test a few obvious and borderline examples, and revise the input if the classifications drift from your actual offer.

Useful output: A table of terms grouped by intent, with review notes and the original data. Keep the decision and change log with your account owner.
